Coverage

The work of the hospital, on one screen.

The language on the screen is the language of the hospital. Patient, appointment, bed, report, bill.

01

Reception and outpatient

Register the patient, book the doctor, and see who is waiting. Doctor hours and same-day procedures sit with the front desk.

02

Wards and beds

See which beds are free and who is admitted. A transfer is accepted by the receiving ward, so the bed list stays accurate.

03

Laboratory

Book a test, enter the result, and print the report. A value that needs prompt attention is marked clearly.

04

Pharmacy

Dispense against a prescription and sell at the counter. Low stock and approaching expiry are shown in advance.

05

Billing

Prepare the bill for a visit or a stay. Record a payment, a deposit, or a refund. An insurer’s share can appear on the same bill, with GST figures for accounts.

06

Patient record

Notes, reports, and previous bills remain with the patient. The next visit begins from the last one.

A visit

From arrival to the bill.

One visit moves from desk to desk. The patient’s name is entered once.

  1. 01

    Reception

    The patient is registered and the doctor is booked.

  2. 02

    Doctor

    The same record is opened. A note is written, and a test or prescription is sent.

  3. 03

    Laboratory and pharmacy

    The report is printed. The prescribed medicines are dispensed.

  4. 04

    Accounts

    The bill is already prepared. Payment is taken and a copy is printed.

Questions

Before you decide.

Does the day’s work need the internet?

No. Patients, bills, and reports are kept on a computer in the hospital. They are not waiting on a website.

Will staff need special computer training?

Staff need to be comfortable typing, as on any billing screen. The words are the ones already used in the hospital: patient, appointment, bed, bill, report.

Can a hospital start with only part of it?

Yes. Reception and billing can be used first. Laboratory, pharmacy, wards, or physiotherapy can be added when that work is in place. A smaller clinic does not have to carry the screens of a large hospital.

Can several people use it at the same time?

Yes. Reception, the laboratory, and the ward can all be signed in together, each from a computer on the hospital network.

Who can see the bills?

The hospital decides. Each person has a separate username. Reception can register patients without opening accounts. Pharmacy sees stock. The owner can see the wider picture.

Where are the records kept?

On a computer in the hospital. A backup copy can be saved as well, so the failure of one machine does not remove the records.

What can be printed for the patient?

Bills, prescriptions, laboratory reports, discharge summaries, and deposit receipts. The hospital name can appear at the top.

Does this page open the program?

No. This page only introduces it. To see the program with your own hospital in mind, speak with the person who looks after KT HEALTH ERP for you.
